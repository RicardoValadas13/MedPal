# MedPal Fitness — Video Prompt Pack 💪

> **The pitch (read this in a confident boardroom voice):**
> "We taught grandma to take her pills on time. The logical next step? Teach Chad to take his creatine on time."
> **MedPal Fitness** — the same obsessive, doctor-grade organisation you trust for prescriptions, now pointed at protein, pre-workout, and *fitness prescriptions* written by actual professionals. Because a missed dose of leucine is technically a medical emergency. (It is not. But it feels like one.)

**Tone:** mock-serious infomercial that slowly realises how ridiculous it is and fully commits anyway. Warm, glossy, a little too proud of itself.
