# MedPal — Video Prompt Pack

**Total runtime:** ~90 seconds across 7 shots  
**Character anchor — Maria:** Woman, early 70s, silver-white hair in a soft bun, warm brown eyes, reading glasses, cream or sage linen blouse. Gentle, slightly tired but kind face. Use a character-sheet reference image across all shots for consistency.  
**Palette anchors:** eggshell #FAF9F5, sage green #49654D, deep charcoal #192830, warm white surfaces, soft natural morning light.  
**No hospital environments. No cold blue tones. Every shot takes place in a home, garden, or warm office.**

---

## MODEL: VEO 3 / VEO 3.1
*(Five-part structure: Cinematography + Subject + Action + Context + Style & Ambiance + Audio)*

---

### SHOT 1 — The problem (≈10 s)

Slow push-in, wide shot with shallow depth of field. A cluttered kitchen counter: four prescription bottles, loose white tablets, a handwritten note with crossed-out medication names, and a cracked weekly pill box — desaturated, slightly cold. A woman in her early seventies with silver-white hair and reading glasses sits at the table in soft morning light. She picks up two bottles, squints at the labels, puts them down, exhales quietly. Warm eggshell kitchen walls, pale oak table. Style: photorealistic, warm cinematic grade. Mood: quiet worry without drama.
Audio: ambient morning — distant birdsong through an open window, the soft clink of a glass. No music. No dialogue.

---

### SHOT 2 — The arrival (≈8 s)

Macro close-up, static camera, then slow pull back. A flat white box sits on the kitchen table. The same woman's hands — warm-toned, slightly aged — lift the lid. Inside: the MedPal Smart Box, translucent lid, charcoal-grey frame, 28 labelled compartments (MON–SUN, MORNING–NIGHT), the MedPal "✚" logo subtly embossed. Morning light catches the transparent lid. She lifts it carefully. Eggshell and sage palette. Style: product photography warmth, photorealistic.
SFX: soft cardboard opening, then a satisfying click as the lid snaps open. Ambient: faint birds. No music yet.

---

### SHOT 3 — Magic Scan (≈12 s)

Over-the-shoulder shot then cut to phone screen insert. The woman holds a smartphone over a printed prescription on the table. The MedPal app camera activates — a soft green scanning ring pulses. Then a phone-screen close-up: medication cards appear one by one — "Lisinopril 10mg · Once a day · Morning" with a green "Identified ✓" chip. She lowers the phone and smiles — small, genuine, not theatrical. Warm morning light. Style: photorealistic, warm and clean.
SFX: a gentle soft chime as each card confirms. Ambient: kitchen hum. No dialogue.

---

### SHOT 4 — Filling the box (≈8 s)

Close-up tracking shot following her hands. She fills each compartment of the Smart Box — pills drop in with a soft click. She snaps the transparent lid shut. Then she holds the phone over the closed box. A brief screen flash: "All slots verified ✓" in sage green. She places the box on the windowsill in the morning light. Macro lens, shallow depth of field, warm backlighting. Style: intimate, tactile, ASMR-adjacent warmth.
SFX: soft sequential clicks of pill compartments closing, final lid snap. Quiet. No music.

---

### SHOT 5 — The daily routine + family (≈12 s)

Split sequence: two shots in one. First — the woman at her kitchen window with a coffee mug, phone buzzes gently on the table. Screen insert: "Good morning, Maria. Your 8:00 AM dose is ready — Lisinopril 20mg." She opens the Smart Box, takes the pill. Cut to — a woman in her 40s at a modern office desk glances at her phone. A quiet notification: "Maria took her morning dose ✓". She exhales, visibly relaxes. Two-shot warmth. Style: cinematic, natural light.
SFX: gentle phone buzz. Ambient: office hum in second shot. No dialogue. Ambient: soft acoustic piano note enters quietly.
Audio: A woman says, "No more guessing."

---

### SHOT 6 — The doctor (≈12 s)

Medium two-shot then cut to screen close-up. A male doctor, early 50s, warm expression, navy shirt, sits across from Maria in a bright, plant-filled office — not a hospital room. He opens a laptop. Screen close-up: a clean MedPal dashboard — adherence chart, 94% in sage green, medication timeline, side effect log. He looks up at her with a nod. She nods back. Style: warm documentary, natural window light, shallow depth of field.
Ambient: soft office, paper shuffle. Piano continues gently.
Dialogue: Doctor says, "Everything looks exactly as it should."

---

### SHOT 7 — Logo reveal + CTA (≈10 s)

Wide shot slowly pulling back, then cut to clean frame. Maria in her garden, watering a plant. Relaxed, unhurried, well. Slow zoom out — she looks at peace. Cut to clean eggshell white frame. The MedPal "✚" mark fades in first, then "MedPal" in deep charcoal. Below in sage green: "Care that understands you." Pause. Then: "Smart Box · App · Medical Team — €19.99" fades in. Bottom corner: a QR code. Style: clean, minimal, confident.
Audio: piano resolves on a single warm note. Then silence.
