# MedPal — Promotional Video Prompt

**Duration:** 60–90 seconds  
**Tone:** Warm, calm, reassuring. Not clinical. Not alarming. Like a trusted friend explaining something important.  
**Palette:** Eggshell whites, sage greens, deep charcoal. Natural morning light throughout.  
**Music:** Soft acoustic piano, building gently. No percussion until the final CTA beat.

---

## Shot-by-Shot

---

### SCENE 1 — The Problem (0:00–0:12)

**Shot:** Close-up of a kitchen counter. A chaotic mess of pill bottles, loose tablets, a handwritten note with crossed-out medication names, and an old weekly pill box with missing compartments.

**Camera:** Slow push-in. Everything slightly out of focus, desaturated.

**Text overlay (fade in):**
> *"Every day, millions of people manage complex medication routines alone."*

**Cut to:** An elderly woman — 70s, kind face, reading glasses — sitting at the kitchen table in the morning light. She picks up three different bottles, squinting at the labels. She looks uncertain. She sighs quietly.

**No dialogue. Just ambient kitchen sounds.**

---

### SCENE 2 — The Arrival (0:12–0:22)

**Shot:** A flat white package arrives on the same kitchen table. The woman opens it slowly.

**Cut to:** Her hands lifting the **MedPal Smart Box** from the packaging. The box is elegant — translucent lid, clean compartment grid, the MedPal logo subtly embossed on the lid. Morning light catches it.

**Camera:** Slow macro shot of the box. Each compartment is colour-labelled: MON, TUE, WED... MORNING, AFTERNOON, EVENING, NIGHT.

**Text overlay:**
> *"Introducing the MedPal Smart Box."*

**Sound:** A soft, satisfying click as she opens the lid.

---

### SCENE 3 — Magic Scan (0:22–0:35)

**Shot:** The woman holds her phone over a printed prescription. The MedPal app is open.

**Screen insert (phone close-up):** The camera activates. A subtle scanning animation. Then — card by card — her medications appear on screen: *Lisinopril 10mg · Once a day · Morning.* A green "Identified ✓" chip appears on each one.

**She smiles slightly. A small, real smile — not theatrical.**

**Voiceover (warm female voice):**
> *"Point your camera at any prescription. MedPal reads it instantly — no typing, no mistakes."*

**Cut to:** Her filling the Smart Box. Each compartment clicks shut. She snaps a photo of the filled box. The app confirms: *"All slots verified ✓"*

---

### SCENE 4 — The Daily Routine (0:35–0:50)

**Shot:** Morning. She's at the window with coffee. Her phone buzzes gently.

**Screen insert:** *"Good morning, Maria. Your 8:00 AM dose is ready — Lisinopril 20mg."* A single "Take Now" button.

**She taps it. Opens the Smart Box. Takes her pill.**

**Cut to:** Her daughter — 40s, at work — glances at her own phone. A quiet notification: *"Maria took her morning dose ✓"*

**The daughter exhales. Relaxed.**

**Voiceover:**
> *"Real-time peace of mind — for the people who matter most."*

---

### SCENE 5 — The Doctor (0:50–1:05)

**Shot:** A doctor's office. Bright, modern. A male doctor — 50s, warm expression — sits across from Maria at a consultation.

**He opens his laptop. The MedPal Doctor Mode dashboard is visible on screen: a clean adherence chart, 94% over 30 days, medication timeline, side effect log.**

**Doctor (to camera, genuine):**
> *"With MedPal, I see exactly how my patient is taking their medication — before they even sit down. No memory gaps. Just real data."*

**Cut to:** Close-up of the dashboard. The chart is clean, green, precise. A "Share Report" button pulses gently.

---

### SCENE 6 — Logo & CTA (1:05–1:20)

**Shot:** Back to Maria. She's in her garden now. Relaxed. Watering a plant. Her phone is in her pocket.

**Slow zoom out. Natural light. She looks well.**

**Cut to:** Clean white frame.

**The MedPal logo animates in — the ✚ mark first, then "MedPal" in deep charcoal.**

**Below it, in sage green:**
> *"Healthcare that understands you."*

**Pause. Then:**

**Text (one line at a time, fading in):**
> 📷 Scan any prescription instantly  
> 📦 Smart Box — verified, every day  
> 🩺 Your doctor, always informed

**Final frame:**

> **MedPal Smart Box — €19.99**  
> Box + App + Medical Support. One-time payment.  
> *(QR code in corner)*  
> **medpal.pt**

**Music resolves. One final soft piano note.**

## Production Notes

- **Cast:** Real people preferred over models. Maria should feel like someone's grandmother, not a stock photo.
- **Hands matter:** Many close-up shots of hands — opening the box, tapping the app, filling compartments. Hands are intimate and trustworthy.
- **No hospital scenes.** The entire video takes place in homes, a kitchen, a garden, a warm doctor's office — never a cold medical environment.
- **App screen inserts:** Use real MedPal UI screens (English version). No mock-ups.
- **Logo timing:** The MedPal ✚ mark should appear briefly in the bottom corner from scene 2 onwards — subtle watermark — so it's embedded in the viewer's memory before the final reveal.
- **Subtitles:** Include Portuguese and English versions. The voiceover can be recorded in both.
